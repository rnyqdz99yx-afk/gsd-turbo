# gsd-turbo Stage 4 L3 — prep ahead and the ROADMAP markup Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Let future phases go through discuss and research while other phases run (`/turbo-prep N` jobs in lane slots), and mark up the ROADMAP so the L1 scheduler knows which phases may run at the same time (`**Areas**:`, `**Gates**:`, `**Parallel with**:` and `.planning/turbo/lanes.json`, written from the analysis of a new agent `turbo-lane-analyst`), with `/turbo-plan-milestone` and `/turbo-new-milestone` to do it for a whole milestone.

**Architecture:** `lib/roadmap-lanes.mjs` reads and writes the three markup lines inside a phase's ROADMAP section without disturbing what GSD reads (Goal, Depends on), and computes a digest of the ROADMAP's scheduling text. `lib/lanes-file.mjs` reads and writes `lanes.json` and decides its freshness; L1's scheduler reads it through `readLanesFile` and `areasOf`. `lib/prep.mjs` tells L1, each tick, which prep and annotate jobs are due (`pendingJobs`), builds their session prompts, and holds the path predicates that L2's planning-only merge applies. A prep job runs the new skill `/turbo-prep`, which follows `/turbo-phase`'s own freshness, discuss and prologue sections (`turbo-run phase-step N --prep` keeps its step record in the slot) and stops before plan. An annotate job runs a short procedure: `turbo-run annotate brief` writes the analyst's input, `turbo-lane-analyst` writes one analysis JSON, and `turbo-run annotate apply` (`lib/annotate.mjs`) validates it and writes the ROADMAP lines, `lanes.json` and, for relaxed dependencies, an ADR (`autonomy: max`) or one owner card (`standard`), then commits. Both jobs end with `turbo-run lane-status … --job prep` / `lane-status annotate …`, which refuse anything outside the job's files.

**Tech Stack:** Node ≥ 20 (ESM `.mjs`, `node:test`, zero npm dependencies), git, Claude Code background sessions (2.1.29x), GSD 1.16.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-10-13-stage-4-design.md` — L3's scope (§11): §6.1 prep, §6.2 the markup and the analyst, §6.3 (the `**` rule only; the mask intersection itself is L1's), §6.4 freshness, §6.5 commands, the facts G3–G5 of §3, the L3 tests of §13 (`lanes.json` and freshness, the ROADMAP markup parse, a prep that touched more than `.planning/phases/<N>-*` refused).

## Decisions this plan makes where the spec is open

- **D1 Prep's step record.** `turbo-run phase-step N --prep` keeps `freshness`, `discuss`, `prologue` in `<localRunDir(root)>/prep-p<N>.json`: in the slot (L0 D3), so the next `prepareSlot` wipes it, and apart from the phase's own `phase-p<N>.json`. The phase's lane later runs those three steps again on the main branch it starts from; with CONTEXT, RESEARCH and the other contracts merged they finish in seconds, and `freshness` re-checks them against the newer `main` (spec §6.1: "шаг `freshness` точечно обновляет устаревшее").
- **D2 Prep never asks the owner.** A prep job is an optimization: every stop of the reused sections becomes `lane-status N failed --job prep`, and the phase's lane does the work itself later. GSD may commit outside the phase directory while prep runs (discuss-phase commits STATE.md); `/turbo-prep`'s **Finish** puts those paths back to the job's base commit before `lane-status N prepped --job prep`, which refuses otherwise. Prologue point 1 (`gates chunked`, a `.planning/config.json` commit) and the `gsdTools` jobs (intel, outside the phase directory) are skipped; the phase's lane runs them.
- **D3 What `roadmap_sha256` hashes.** The spec's "sha256 ROADMAP" is the sha256 of the ROADMAP's **scheduling text**: per phase section (in order) its number, name, Goal line, Depends on line and Requirements line (`roadmapDigest`). GSD's own edits at plan and phase completion (checkboxes, `**Plans**:`, the Progress table) and turbo's markup lines leave it unchanged; the spec's triggers (a phase added or removed, a dependency edited) change it. A hash of the raw bytes would go stale at every `phase complete` the supervisor commits and hold L1 to one lane after every merge. `readLanesFile(root).fresh` is `roadmapDigest(current ROADMAP) === stored`.
- **D4 Sections.** A phase section starts at a line-anchored `##`–`####` `Phase N: Name` heading (GSD 1.16 `collectAnalyzePhases`) and ends at the next phase heading, at the next heading of its level or above, or at a `<details>` line. Headings inside `<details>` blocks (GSD's shipped milestones) are not sections. A section's **key** is the sha256 of its number, name, Goal, Depends on and Requirements values; `lanes.json` stores it per phase (`section`), and a phase whose key changed is stale (§6.4).
- **D5 Where the lines go.** Right after the `**Depends on**:` line; without one, after the Goal field's last line (GSD's continuation rule); without a Goal, right after the heading. Existing markup lines of the section are removed first, so writing is idempotent. `**Gates**:` and `**Parallel with**:` are left out when empty. `**Parallel with**:` lists plain phase numbers (`4, 7`, never `Phase 4`: no GSD reader may ever take them for references), from the analyst's `ok` pairs minus pairs linked by a dependency chain. Line endings (CRLF or LF) are kept.
- **D6 Area masks.** Globs from the repository root (`*` one segment, `**` any), forward slashes; refused: braces (`{a,b}` would break the comma list), commas, absolute paths, `..`, anything under `.planning`. `**` alone is allowed and means everything.
- **D7 `lanes.json` shape.** The spec's fields plus `v: 1`, `annotated_at`, `phases.<N>.section` (D4) and `proposal` (D9). Phase keys are normalized ids; pairs `[a, b]` with `comparePhase(a, b) < 0`. Unparseable values read conservatively: an entry without valid areas or with a confidence other than `high` reads as `low` (`**`).
- **D8 Who writes the markup.** The analyst agent writes only one JSON file (`<localRunDir>/annotate/analysis.json`); `turbo-run annotate apply` validates it (refusing with a list of problems and writing nothing) and writes the ROADMAP lines, `lanes.json` and the decision record, and commits them in one commit. Targets: every unfinished phase without `lanes.json`, else only the stale ones (`stalePhases`); markup of the other unfinished phases and their pairs is kept.
- **D9 The dependency card.** With `autonomy: standard` the relaxations go to the owner as one card asked under the pseudo-phase id `annotate` (`ANNOTATE_PHASE`), with the options «Remove all» (recommended) and «Leave as is»; the spec's «принять выборочно» is the owner's own words (the context says so), read by the next annotate session, which picks the proposal ids (`--relax r1,r3`). Nothing waits for the card: the markup job ends with the strict dependencies, and `pendingJobs` queues a new annotate job once the answer is in. Templated card text (en, ru) holds no file names, so the plain-words check passes. Phase numbers stay in the card.
- **D10 Decision records.** `.planning/decisions/turbo-<yyyy-mm-dd>-relaxed-dependencies[-k].md` when that directory exists, else a section appended to `.planning/turbo/DECISIONS.md`: per phase, Was (the Depends on line as written), Now, Why (kind and reason), Roll back. Written only for relaxations that are applied (autonomy max, or accepted by the owner).
- **D11 Job statuses.** Where L1 reads them (L1 plan D3): a lane record through `writeLaneStatus`, `p<N>.json` for a prep job (a phase's prep and its lane never run at once) and `pannotate.json` for the annotate job, with L1's statuses `prepped` and `annotated`. Each kind accepts only its own set (`JOB_STATUSES`). Written by `turbo-run lane-status <N> <status> --job prep` and `turbo-run lane-status annotate <status>`; `prepped` and `annotated` refuse a dirty tree, any changed path outside the job's predicate (net diff from the slot record's `job.base.sha`), a prep without CONTEXT.md and an annotate whose `lanes.json` is not fresh. Every annotate status also writes `run/annotate-attempt.json` with the ROADMAP digest, so a failed markup is not retried on the same ROADMAP.
- **D17 Finishing a job.** L1 hands a lane whose record reads `prepped`/`annotated` to `finishJob(ctx, lane)` (L1's plugin contract): it runs L2's `mergePlanningOnly` with `allowedFor(job)`, then L0's `releaseSlot` — `merged` on a merge, `failed` (and the job's record `failed`) on a refusal; `wait` and `needs-owner` pass through untouched (L2 plan D17: L3 releases the slot).
- **D12 `pendingJobs`.** Prep for every unfinished phase of the run's range that has no CONTEXT.md in the control root, no lanes entry and no prep status `failed` or `prepped`, by `comparePhase`, capped at `maxPrep(config)` minus the prep lanes running; then one annotate job only when no prep is due or running and no annotate lane runs. Nothing in `lanes.mode: "checkout"` or in safe mode. A failed prep is never retried automatically.
- **D13 Plan-only start.** `/turbo-plan-milestone` runs `turbo-run start --plan-only`; the flag and the end-of-preparation rule are L1's (`start`, `supervisor.json`, the scheduler loop), pinned in the last section. This plan writes no supervisor code.
- **D14 No `/turbo-annotate` command.** The spec names the agent and a background session, no command: the annotate session runs a prompt (`jobUserPrompt({ kind: 'annotate' })`) whose steps are three `turbo-run` calls and one agent dispatch.
- **D15 Install.** `install.mjs` already installs every `skills/turbo-*` directory and `agents/turbo-*` file, and its manifest uninstalls them; it is not changed. A test pins the new files in and out.

## Review Focus

1. **A real ROADMAP's variety**: CRLF line endings, the `**Depends on:**` label form, a phase without a Depends on line, a multi-line Goal, a shipped milestone's phases inside `<details>`, a Progress table after the last phase. Expected: the lines land after Depends on (or the Goal), GSD reads the same Goal and Depends on as before, CRLF stays CRLF, `<details>` phases are never marked up. Pinned in Task 1 (`G3: …`, `CRLF stays CRLF …`, `parse …`).
2. **GSD completing a phase while the markup stands** (checkbox in the summary list, `**Plans**: 2/2`, a Progress row). Expected: `lanes.json` stays fresh and no annotate job is queued; a changed Depends on, a new phase or a renamed phase makes only the touched phases stale. Pinned in Task 1 (`the digest …`) and Task 2 (`fresh survives …`).
3. **An analyst output that is wrong** (a brace mask, `.planning/**`, an unknown gate, a phase that is no target, a relaxation that drops a dependency the phase does not have, a pair naming a finished phase). Expected: refused with every problem listed, ROADMAP and `lanes.json` byte-identical, no commit. Pinned in Task 6 (`an invalid analysis is refused …`).
4. **GSD committing STATE.md during prep**, or leaving an uncommitted file. Expected: `lane-status N prepped --job prep` refuses and names the paths and the base; after putting STATE.md back at the base it passes, and the job status lands in the control root only. Pinned in Task 4 (`prepped refuses …`).
5. **The owner's answer to the dependency card**: accept all, leave as is, own words naming some ids, an unknown id, a Depends on line edited after the card was asked. Expected: only accepted ids applied (an edited line skipped, never overwritten), an ADR only for applied ones, the question closed, no re-queue afterwards; an unknown id refused. Pinned in Task 6 (`standard autonomy asks one card …`, `applyAnswer refuses …`).

```js
// Phase-word dependency scan of phase-id.cjs (G4).
export function gsdSection(text, n) {
  const at = text.search(new RegExp(`^ {0,3}#{2,4}\\s*Phase\\s+${n.replace('.', '\\.')}\\s*:`, 'm'));
  if (at < 0) return null;
  const rest = text.slice(at);
  const next = rest.slice(1).search(/\n {0,3}#{2,4}\s+Phase\s+[A-Za-z]?\d/i);
  return next < 0 ? rest : rest.slice(0, next + 1);
}

export function gsdDepends(section) {
  return /\*\*Depends on(?::\*\*|\*\*:)\s*([^\n]+)/i.exec(section)?.[1].trim() ?? null;
}

export function gsdGoal(section) {
  const m = /^[ \t]*\*\*Goal(?::\*\*|\*\*\s*:?)\s*([^\n]+)/im.exec(section);
  if (!m) return null;
  const out = [m[1].trim()];
  const rest = section.slice(m.index + m[0].length).split('\n');
  for (let i = 0; i < rest.length; i++) {
    const raw = rest[i];
    if (i === 0 && !raw.trim()) continue;
    if (!raw.trim() || /^\s*[-*+]\s/.test(raw) || /^\s*\*\*[A-Za-z][A-Za-z ]*:?(\*\*)?:?\s/.test(raw) || /^\s*#{1,4}\s/.test(raw) || /^\s*\|/.test(raw) || /^\s*(?:`{3,}|~{3,})/.test(raw)) break;
    out.push(raw.trim());
  }
  return out.join(' ').trim();
}

export function gsdDeps(value) {
  return [...String(value ?? '').matchAll(/\bphases?\s+(\d+[A-Z]?(?:\.\d+)*)/gi)].map((m) => m[1]);
}
```

```js
import crypto from 'node:crypto';
import { normalizePhaseId } from './gsd.mjs';

// spec §6.2, G3: turbo's markup lines in a phase's ROADMAP section, right after **Depends on**:. GSD 1.16 reads none
// of them, and each one ends the Goal field (extractPhaseFieldMultiline stops at a **Label**: line).
export const LANE_FIELDS = Object.freeze({ areas: 'Areas', gates: 'Gates', parallelWith: 'Parallel with' });

// GSD 1.16 roadmap.cjs collectAnalyzePhases: a line-anchored `## Phase N: Name` (2 to 4 #, an optional ( ) tag).
const HEADING = /^ {0,3}(#{2,4})\s*Phase\s+([A-Za-z]?\d+[A-Z]?(?:[.-]\d+)*)(?:\s*\([^)\n]{0,200}\))?\s*:\s*(.*?)\s*$/i;
const ANY_HEADING = /^ {0,3}(#{1,6})\s/;
const DETAILS = /<details\b/i;
const DETAILS_END = /<\/details>/i;
// GSD's label shapes: **X**:, **X:**, **X** :
const fieldRe = (label) => new RegExp(`^([ \\t]*\\*\\*${label}(?::\\*\\*|\\*\\*[ \\t]*:?))[ \\t]*(.*)$`, 'i');
const GOAL = fieldRe('Goal');
const DEPENDS = fieldRe('Depends on');
const REQUIREMENTS = fieldRe('Requirements');
const OURS = Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(LANE_FIELDS).map(([k, label]) => [k, fieldRe(label)]));
const isOurs = (line) => Object.values(OURS).some((re) => re.test(line));

const split = (text) => String(text).split(/\r?\n/);
const eolOf = (text) => (/\r\n/.test(text) ? '\r\n' : '\n');
const sha = (s) => crypto.createHash('sha256').update(s).digest('hex');
const list = (v) => v.split(',').map((x) => x.trim()).filter(Boolean);

// GSD's continuation rule (extractPhaseFieldMultiline): a field goes on until a blank line, a list item, another
// **Label**, a heading, a table row or a fence. Returns the index of the field's last line.
const STOPS = [/^\s*$/, /^\s*[-*+]\s/, /^\s*\*\*[A-Za-z][A-Za-z ]*:?(\*\*)?:?\s/, /^\s*#{1,4}\s/, /^\s*\|/, /^\s*(?:`{3,}|~{3,})/];
function fieldEnd(body, at) {
  let i = at;
  while (i + 1 < body.length && !STOPS.some((re) => re.test(body[i + 1]))) i++;
  return i;
}

// D4: every phase section outside <details> blocks (GSD keeps shipped milestones there), from its heading to the next
// phase heading, the next heading of its level or above, or a <details> line.
export function parseRoadmapPhases(text) {
  const all = split(text);
  const heads = [];
  let details = 0;
  all.forEach((l, i) => {
    if (DETAILS.test(l)) details++;
    const m = HEADING.exec(l);
    if (m && details === 0) heads.push({ i, level: m[1].length, number: normalizePhaseId(m[2]), name: m[3].replace(/\(INSERTED\)/i, '').trim() });
    if (DETAILS_END.test(l)) details = Math.max(0, details - 1);
  });
  return heads.map((h) => {
    let end = all.length;
    for (let j = h.i + 1; j < all.length; j++) {
      const a = ANY_HEADING.exec(all[j]);
      if (HEADING.test(all[j]) || DETAILS.test(all[j]) || (a && a[1].length <= h.level)) {
        end = j;
        break;
      }
    }
    const body = all.slice(h.i + 1, end);
    const value = (re) => {
      const line = body.find((x) => re.test(x));
      return line === undefined ? null : re.exec(line)[2].trim();
    };
    const ours = (k) => {
      const v = value(OURS[k]);
      return v === null ? null : list(v);
    };
    const goal = value(GOAL);
    const dependsOn = value(DEPENDS);
    const requirements = value(REQUIREMENTS);
    const parallel = ours('parallelWith');
    return {
      number: h.number, name: h.name, start: h.i, end, goal, dependsOn, requirements,
      areas: ours('areas'), gates: ours('gates'), parallelWith: parallel && parallel.map(normalizePhaseId),
      key: sha(JSON.stringify([h.number, h.name, goal, dependsOn, requirements])),
    };
  });
}

// D3: the sha256 of the ROADMAP's scheduling text, lanes.json's roadmap_sha256 (spec §6.4). GSD's own completion edits
// (checkboxes, Plans, Progress) and turbo's lines leave it unchanged; line endings never count.
export function roadmapDigest(text) {
  return sha(JSON.stringify(parseRoadmapPhases(text).map((s) => [s.number, s.key])));
}

// G4: GSD reads a dependency only with the word Phase.
export const depsText = (deps) => (deps.length ? deps.map((d) => `Phase ${d}`).join(', ') : 'Nothing');

function sectionOf(text, phase) {
  const s = parseRoadmapPhases(text).find((x) => x.number === normalizePhaseId(String(phase)));
  if (!s) throw new Error(`phase ${phase} has no section in the ROADMAP`);
  return s;
}

// D5: after Depends on; else after the Goal field's last line; else right after the heading (-1).
function anchor(body) {
  const d = body.findIndex((l) => DEPENDS.test(l));
  if (d >= 0) return d;
  const g = body.findIndex((l) => GOAL.test(l));
  return g >= 0 ? fieldEnd(body, g) : -1;
}

function rebuild(text, s, body) {
  const all = split(text);
  return [...all.slice(0, s.start + 1), ...body, ...all.slice(s.end)].join(eolOf(text));
}

export function writeLaneLines(text, phase, { areas = [], gates = [], parallelWith = [] } = {}) {
  const s = sectionOf(text, phase);
  const body = split(text).slice(s.start + 1, s.end).filter((l) => !isOurs(l));
  const add = [
    areas.length ? `**${LANE_FIELDS.areas}**: ${areas.join(', ')}` : null,
    gates.length ? `**${LANE_FIELDS.gates}**: ${gates.join(', ')}` : null,
    parallelWith.length ? `**${LANE_FIELDS.parallelWith}**: ${parallelWith.join(', ')}` : null,
  ].filter(Boolean);
  body.splice(anchor(body) + 1, 0, ...add);
  return rebuild(text, s, body);
}

// spec §6.2: a relaxed dependency rewrites the line in the Phase 3, Phase 4 form, keeping its label form.
export function setDependsOn(text, phase, deps) {
  const s = sectionOf(text, phase);
  const body = split(text).slice(s.start + 1, s.end);
  const d = body.findIndex((l) => DEPENDS.test(l));
  if (d >= 0) body[d] = body[d].replace(DEPENDS, (_, label) => `${label} ${depsText(deps)}`);
  else if (deps.length) {
    const g = body.findIndex((l) => GOAL.test(l));
    body.splice(g >= 0 ? fieldEnd(body, g) + 1 : 0, 0, `**Depends on**: ${depsText(deps)}`);
  } else return text;
  return rebuild(text, s, body);
}
```

```js
import { normalizePhaseId } from './gsd.mjs';
import { comparePhase, isFinished } from './scheduler.mjs';
import { parseRoadmapPhases, roadmapDigest } from './roadmap-lanes.mjs';

// spec §6.2: the machine-readable markup next to the ROADMAP lines; L1's scheduler reads it (readLanesFile, areasOf).
export const LANES_REL = '.planning/turbo/lanes.json';
export const ROADMAP_REL = '.planning/ROADMAP.md';
export const GATES = Object.freeze(['prod-deploy', 'owner-live', 'owner-signature', 'packaged-build']);
export const CONFIDENCE = Object.freeze(['high', 'low']);
export const PAIR_STATES = Object.freeze(['conflict', 'ok']);
// spec §5, §6.3: a phase without markup touches everything
export const EVERYTHING = '**';
const GATE_RE = /^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]{0,39}$/;

export const lanesFile = (root) => path.join(root, ...LANES_REL.split('/'));
export const roadmapFile = (root) => path.join(root, ...ROADMAP_REL.split('/'));

export function readRoadmap(root) {
  try {
    return fs.readFileSync(roadmapFile(root), 'utf8');
  } catch {
    return null;
  }
}

const isObj = (v) => v !== null && typeof v === 'object' && !Array.isArray(v);
const strings = (v) => (Array.isArray(v) ? v.filter((x) => typeof x === 'string' && x.trim()).map((x) => x.trim()) : null);

// D7: read conservatively: no valid areas, or any confidence but high, is low (counted as everything).
function entryOf(e) {
  const areas = strings(e?.areas);
  const valid = Boolean(areas && areas.length);
  return {
    areas: valid ? areas : [EVERYTHING],
    gates: [...new Set((strings(e?.gates) ?? []).filter((g) => GATE_RE.test(g)))],
    confidence: valid && e?.confidence === 'high' ? 'high' : 'low',
    section: typeof e?.section === 'string' ? e.section : null,
  };
}

function pairOf(p) {
  if (!Array.isArray(p) || p.length < 3 || !PAIR_STATES.includes(p[2])) return null;
  const [a, b] = [normalizePhaseId(String(p[0])), normalizePhaseId(String(p[1]))];
  if (a === b) return null;
  const [x, y] = [a, b].sort(comparePhase);
  return [x, y, p[2], typeof p[3] === 'string' ? p[3] : ''];
}

const proposalOf = (p) => (isObj(p) && Array.isArray(p.relax) && ['asked', 'applied', 'declined'].includes(p.state) ? p : null);

export function readLanesRaw(root) {
  const raw = readJson(lanesFile(root), null);
  if (!isObj(raw) || !isObj(raw.phases) || typeof raw.roadmap_sha256 !== 'string') return null;
  const phases = {};
  for (const [n, e] of Object.entries(raw.phases)) phases[normalizePhaseId(n)] = entryOf(e);
  return {
    roadmapSha256: raw.roadmap_sha256,
    annotatedAt: typeof raw.annotated_at === 'string' ? raw.annotated_at : null,
    phases,
    pairs: (Array.isArray(raw.pairs) ? raw.pairs : []).map(pairOf).filter(Boolean),
    proposal: proposalOf(raw.proposal),
  };
}

// The pinned interface (L1): fresh = the digest of the ROADMAP on disk equals the stored one (D3). A phase whose
// section changed since its markup (or that left the ROADMAP) is left out, so it counts as everything (spec §6.4).
export function readLanesFile(root) {
  const raw = readLanesRaw(root);
  if (!raw) return null;
  const text = readRoadmap(root);
  const keys = new Map((text === null ? [] : parseRoadmapPhases(text)).map((s) => [s.number, s.key]));
  return {
    fresh: text !== null && roadmapDigest(text) === raw.roadmapSha256,
    roadmapSha256: raw.roadmapSha256,
    phases: Object.fromEntries(Object.entries(raw.phases).filter(([n, e]) => keys.get(n) === e.section).map(([n, e]) => [n, { areas: e.areas, gates: e.gates, confidence: e.confidence }])),
    pairs: raw.pairs,
  };
}

// The roadmap digest and each phase's section key come from the ROADMAP on disk unless given.
export function writeLanesFile(root, data) {
  const text = readRoadmap(root);
  if (text === null) throw new Error(`no ${ROADMAP_REL}: nothing to mark up`);
  const keys = new Map(parseRoadmapPhases(text).map((s) => [s.number, s.key]));
  const phases = {};
  for (const n of Object.keys(data.phases || {}).sort((a, b) => comparePhase(normalizePhaseId(a), normalizePhaseId(b)))) {
    const id = normalizePhaseId(n);
    const e = entryOf(data.phases[n]);
    phases[id] = { areas: e.areas, gates: e.gates, confidence: e.confidence, section: e.section ?? keys.get(id) ?? null };
  }
  const out = {
    v: 1,
    roadmap_sha256: data.roadmapSha256 ?? roadmapDigest(text),
    annotated_at: data.annotatedAt ?? new Date().toISOString(),
    phases,
    pairs: (data.pairs || []).map(pairOf).filter(Boolean),
    proposal: data.proposal ?? null,
  };
  writeJsonAtomic(lanesFile(root), out);
  return out;
}

// spec §6.2: a phase missing from the markup, or with low confidence, counts as everything (L1's scheduler).
export function areasOf(lanes, phase) {
  const e = lanes?.phases?.[normalizePhaseId(String(phase))];
  return e && e.confidence === 'high' ? e.areas : [EVERYTHING];
}
export const gatesOf = (lanes, phase) => lanes?.phases?.[normalizePhaseId(String(phase))]?.gates ?? [];

// The gate vocabulary of spec §6.2 plus the project's own lanes.gates names.
export function projectGates(config) {
  const own = Array.isArray(config?.lanes?.gates) ? config.lanes.gates.filter((g) => typeof g === 'string' && GATE_RE.test(g)) : [];
  return [...new Set([...GATES, ...own])];
}

// spec §6.4: the unfinished phases with a ROADMAP section that have no markup or whose section changed since.
export function stalePhases(root, phases) {
  const text = readRoadmap(root);
  if (text === null) return [];
  const raw = readLanesRaw(root);
  const keys = new Map(parseRoadmapPhases(text).map((s) => [s.number, s.key]));
  return phases
    .filter((p) => !isFinished(p) && keys.has(p.number))
    .filter((p) => raw?.phases[p.number]?.section !== keys.get(p.number))
    .map((p) => p.number)
    .sort(comparePhase);
}
```

```js
import { execFileSync } from 'node:child_process';
import { dirNameMatchesPhase } from './phase-files.mjs';
import { LANES_REL, ROADMAP_REL } from './lanes-file.mjs';

// spec §6.2 (D9): the owner's dependency card lives under this pseudo-phase id; it is never a phase.
export const ANNOTATE_PHASE = 'annotate';
export const DECISIONS_DIR_REL = '.planning/decisions';
export const DECISIONS_FILE_REL = '.planning/turbo/DECISIONS.md';

const segments = (rel) => String(rel).replace(/\\/g, '/').split('/');
const unsafe = (parts) => parts.some((s) => s === '' || s === '.' || s === '..');

// spec §6.1: a prep branch changes only .planning/phases/<N>-*/** (files inside the phase's own directory).
export function prepAllowed(phase) {
  return (rel) => {
    const parts = segments(rel);
    return !unsafe(parts) && parts.length >= 4 && parts[0] === '.planning' && parts[1] === 'phases' && dirNameMatchesPhase(parts[2], phase);
  };
}

// spec §6.2: an annotate branch changes only the ROADMAP, lanes.json and the decision files.
export function annotateAllowed() {
  return (rel) => {
    const parts = segments(rel);
    if (unsafe(parts)) return false;
    const p = parts.join('/');
    return p === ROADMAP_REL || p === LANES_REL || p === DECISIONS_FILE_REL || (p.startsWith(`${DECISIONS_DIR_REL}/`) && parts.length > 2);
  };
}

// L2's mergePlanningOnly(ctx, lane, { allowed }) gets this predicate for a prep or annotate lane.
export function allowedFor(job) {
  if (job?.kind === 'prep') return prepAllowed(job.phase);
  if (job?.kind === 'annotate') return annotateAllowed();
  throw new Error(`no planning-only merge for a ${job?.kind} job`);
}

export const gitIn = (root) => (args) => String(execFileSync('git', args, { cwd: root, encoding: 'utf8', windowsHide: true, timeout: 60000, stdio: ['ignore', 'pipe', 'pipe'] })).replace(/\r/g, '');

// What would keep this job's branch from a planning-only merge: uncommitted paths (a merge takes commits only), and the
// paths the branch changed since base (net: a path put back is no change) that the job may not change.
export function outOfScope(root, job, { base = null, git = gitIn(root) } = {}) {
  const allowed = allowedFor(job);
  const dirty = git(['status', '--porcelain', '--untracked-files=all']).split('\n').filter(Boolean).map((l) => l.slice(3));
  const changed = base ? git(['diff', '--name-only', '--no-renames', base, 'HEAD']).split('\n').filter(Boolean) : [];
  return { dirty, outside: changed.filter((p) => !allowed(p)) };
}
```

```js
// Positional arguments and the values of the flags named.
function parse(args, valued) {
  const pos = [];
  const values = new Map();
  for (let i = 0; i < args.length; i++) {
    if (valued.includes(args[i])) values.set(args[i], args[++i] ?? '');
    else if (!args[i].startsWith('--')) pos.push(args[i]);
  }
  return { pos, values };
}

// D11: what keeps a job from its last status (prepped, annotated), or null. The base is the slot record's (L0).
export function finishBlocker(root, job, deps = {}) {
  const base = isSlot(root) ? readSlotRecord(root)?.job?.base?.sha ?? null : null;
  const { dirty, outside } = outOfScope(root, job, { base, ...(deps.git ? { git: deps.git } : {}) });
  if (dirty.length) return `uncommitted changes: ${dirty.join(', ')}; commit them or put them back first`;
  if (outside.length) {
    const where = job.kind === 'prep' ? `the phase directory of phase ${job.phase}` : 'the markup files';
    return `outside ${where}: ${outside.join(', ')} (base ${base}); put each back as it is at the base, commit, and run this again`;
  }
  if (job.kind === 'prep') {
    const dir = findPhaseDir(root, job.phase);
    if (!dir || !phaseArtifacts(dir).context) return `phase ${job.phase} has no CONTEXT.md: prep is not done`;
  }
  if (job.kind === 'annotate' && !readLanesFile(root)?.fresh) return 'lanes.json is missing or does not match the ROADMAP: run turbo-run annotate apply first';
  return null;
}

// turbo-run lane-status <N> <status> --job prep | lane-status annotate <status> (spec §6.1, §6.2).
export function jobLaneStatus(args, { root, out = stdoutLine, err = stderrLine, deps = {} } = {}) {
  const { pos, values } = parse(args, ['--job', '--reason', '--project']);
  const annotate = pos[0] === 'annotate';
  const kind = annotate ? 'annotate' : values.get('--job');
  const status = pos[1];
  const bad = !root || pos.length !== 2 || !Object.hasOwn(JOB_STATUSES, kind) || !JOB_STATUSES[kind].includes(status)
    || (annotate && values.has('--job') && values.get('--job') !== 'annotate') || (!annotate && !PHASE_ID.test(pos[0] ?? ''));
  if (bad) {
    err(`usage: turbo-run ${JOB_USAGE}`);
    return 2;
  }
  const job = { kind, phase: annotate ? null : normalizePhaseId(pos[0]) };
  try {
    if (status === 'prepped' || status === 'annotated') {
      const why = finishBlocker(root, job, deps);
      if (why) {
        err(`refused: ${why}`);
        return 1;
      }
    }
    writeJobStatus(root, job, status, { reason: values.get('--reason') || '' });
    if (kind === 'annotate') {
      const text = readRoadmap(root);
      writeAnnotateAttempt(root, { status, roadmapSha256: text === null ? null : roadmapDigest(text) });
    }
    out(kind === 'annotate' ? `annotate: ${status}` : `prep ${job.phase}: ${status}`);
    return 0;
  } catch (e) {
    err(`turbo-run lane-status: ${firstLine(e)}`);
    return 1;
  }
}
```

```js
// spec §6.1, §10: max_prep (default max_lanes).

// spec §6.1: a phase without CONTEXT.md in the control root still needs prep.
export function needsPrep(root, phase) {
  const dir = findPhaseDir(root, phase);
  return !dir || !phaseArtifacts(dir).context;

// spec §6.2, §6.4 (D9, D12): why the ROADMAP needs markup, or null. No lanes.json, stale phases, or the owner's answer to
// the dependency card that no markup applied yet; a markup that failed on this same ROADMAP is not tried again.
export function annotateReason(root, phases) {
  const text = readRoadmap(root);
  if (text === null) return null;
  const last = readAnnotateAttempt(root);
  if (last?.status === 'failed' && last.roadmapSha256 === roadmapDigest(text)) return null;
  const raw = readLanesRaw(root);
  if (raw?.proposal?.state === 'asked' && raw.proposal.id && liveAnswer(readAnswers(root, ANNOTATE_PHASE), raw.proposal.id)) return 'answer';
  return stalePhases(root, phases).length ? 'phases' : null;
}

// spec §6.1, §6.2 (D12): the prep and annotate jobs due now, in launch order. L1 calls it every tick with the lanes after
// this tick's phase launches (phases first) and starts the jobs in free trusted slots. Reads files only.
export function pendingJobs(ctx, phases) {
  const { root, config = {}, lanes = {}, range = null, mode = 'full' } = ctx;
  if (mode !== 'full' || laneMode(config) !== 'slots') return [];
  const entries = Object.values(lanes || {}).filter(isObj);
  const busy = new Set(entries.filter((e) => e.phase !== null && e.phase !== undefined).map((e) => String(e.phase)));
  const runningPrep = entries.filter((e) => e.job === 'prep').length;
  const annotating = entries.some((e) => e.job === 'annotate');
  const prep = phases
    .filter((p) => !isFinished(p) && inRange(p.number, range) && !busy.has(p.number))
    .filter((p) => !['failed', 'prepped'].includes(readJobStatus(root, { kind: 'prep', phase: p.number })?.status))
    .filter((p) => needsPrep(root, p.number))
    .sort((a, b) => comparePhase(a.number, b.number))
    .map((p) => ({ kind: 'prep', phase: p.number }));
  const out = prep.slice(0, Math.max(0, maxPrep(config) - runningPrep));
  if (prep.length || runningPrep || annotating) return out;
  return annotateReason(root, phases) ? [{ kind: 'annotate', phase: null }] : [];
}

// D17, L1's plugin contract: a job lane whose record reads prepped or annotated. L2's planning-only merge with this
// job's predicate, then L0's releaseSlot: merged on a merge; failed (and the job's record failed) on a refusal; wait
// and needs-owner pass through untouched. deps.merge and deps.release replace L2's and L0's functions (tests).
export async function finishJob(ctx, lane, deps = {}) {
  const job = { kind: lane.job, phase: lane.phase ?? null };
  const allowed = allowedFor(job);
  const merge = deps.merge ?? (await import('./merge.mjs')).mergePlanningOnly;
  const release = deps.release ?? (await import('./slots.mjs')).releaseSlot;
  const r = await merge(ctx, lane, { allowed });
  if (r.outcome === 'merged') release(ctx.root, lane.slot, { outcome: 'merged' });
  else if (r.outcome === 'refused') {
    writeJobStatus(ctx.root, job, 'failed', { reason: `merge refused: ${r.reason ?? 'unknown reason'}` });
    release(ctx.root, lane.slot, { outcome: 'failed' });
  }
  return r;
}
```

```js
// A plan's files_modified (GSD frontmatter): an inline [a, b] list or a block list of - items.
export function filesModified(text) {
  const fm = /^---\r?\n([\s\S]*?)\r?\n---/.exec(String(text));
  if (!fm) return [];
  const lines = fm[1].split(/\r?\n/);
  const at = lines.findIndex((l) => /^files_modified:/.test(l));
  if (at < 0) return [];
  const unq = (s) => s.trim().replace(/^["']|["']$/g, '');
  const inline = /^files_modified:\s*\[(.*)\]\s*$/.exec(lines[at]);
  if (inline) return inline[1].split(',').map(unq).filter(Boolean);
  const out = [];
  for (const l of lines.slice(at + 1)) {
    const m = /^\s+-\s+(.*)$/.exec(l);
    if (!m) break;
    out.push(unq(m[1]));
  }
  return out;
}

export function validateAnalysis(a, { targets, unfinished, gates, deps }) {
  if (!isObj(a) || !isObj(a.phases)) return ['the analysis is not a JSON object with a phases object'];
  const problems = [];
  for (const [n, e] of Object.entries(a.phases)) {
    if (!targets.includes(id(n))) problems.push(`phase ${n} is not a phase to mark up (${targets.join(', ') || 'none'})`);
    if (!isObj(e)) {
      problems.push(`phase ${n}: not an object`);
      continue;
    }
    if (!Array.isArray(e.areas) || !e.areas.length || e.areas.some((m) => typeof m !== 'string' || !m)) problems.push(`phase ${n}: areas must be a non-empty list of path masks`);
    else for (const m of e.areas) for (const [re, why] of MASK_BAD) if (re.test(m)) problems.push(`phase ${n}: area ${m} ${why}`);
    if (!Array.isArray(e.gates) || e.gates.some((g) => !gates.includes(g))) problems.push(`phase ${n}: gates must be a list of ${gates.join(', ')}`);
    if (!CONFIDENCE.includes(e.confidence)) problems.push(`phase ${n}: confidence must be high or low`);
  }
  if (a.pairs !== undefined && !Array.isArray(a.pairs)) problems.push('pairs must be a list');
  for (const p of Array.isArray(a.pairs) ? a.pairs : []) {
    if (!Array.isArray(p) || p.length !== 4) {
      problems.push(`pair ${JSON.stringify(p)}: must be [phase, phase, conflict or ok, reason]`);
      continue;
    }
    const [x, y, s, r] = p;
    if (![x, y].every((v) => unfinished.includes(id(v))) || id(x) === id(y)) problems.push(`pair ${x}/${y}: two different unfinished phases`);
    else if (![x, y].some((v) => targets.includes(id(v)))) problems.push(`pair ${x}/${y}: names no phase to mark up`);
    if (!PAIR_STATES.includes(s)) problems.push(`pair ${x}/${y}: state must be conflict or ok`);
    if (typeof r !== 'string') problems.push(`pair ${x}/${y}: the reason must be text`);
  }
  if (a.relax !== undefined && !Array.isArray(a.relax)) problems.push('relax must be a list');
  for (const [i, r] of (Array.isArray(a.relax) ? a.relax : []).entries()) {
    const n = id(r?.phase);
    const have = deps.get(n) ?? [];
    if (!unfinished.includes(n)) problems.push(`relax ${i + 1}: phase ${r?.phase} is not an unfinished phase`);
    if (!Array.isArray(r?.drop) || !r.drop.length || r.drop.some((d) => !have.includes(id(d)))) problems.push(`relax ${i + 1}: drop must name dependencies phase ${n} has (${have.join(', ') || 'none'})`);
    if (!RELAX_KINDS.includes(r?.kind)) problems.push(`relax ${i + 1}: kind must be prose or imaginary`);
    if (typeof r?.reason !== 'string' || !r.reason.trim()) problems.push(`relax ${i + 1}: needs a reason`);
  }
  return problems;
}

// The kept pairs of phases that are not marked up again, plus the analysis's, one per pair of phases.
function mergePairs(old, given, sc) {
  const out = new Map();
  const keep = (n) => sc.unfinished.includes(n) && !sc.targets.includes(n);
  for (const [a, b, s, r] of old) if (keep(a) && keep(b)) out.set(`${a}|${b}`, [a, b, s, r]);
  for (const p of given) {
    const [a, b] = [id(p[0]), id(p[1])].sort(comparePhase);
    out.set(`${a}|${b}`, [a, b, p[2], p[3]]);
  }
  return [...out.values()].sort((x, y) => comparePhase(x[0], y[0]) || comparePhase(x[1], y[1]));
}

// a depends on b, directly or through other phases
function reaches(deps, a, b, seen = new Set()) {
  for (const d of deps.get(a) ?? []) {
    if (d === b) return true;
    if (!seen.has(d)) {
      seen.add(d);
      if (reaches(deps, d, b, seen)) return true;
    }
  }
  return false;
}

// D5: the lines of every phase with markup; Parallel with = the ok pairs no dependency chain links.
function markLines(text, entries, pairs, deps) {
  let out = text;
  for (const n of Object.keys(entries).sort(comparePhase)) {
    const parallelWith = pairs
      .filter((p) => p[2] === 'ok' && (p[0] === n || p[1] === n))
      .map((p) => (p[0] === n ? p[1] : p[0]))
      .filter((m) => !reaches(deps, n, m) && !reaches(deps, m, n))
      .sort(comparePhase);
    out = writeLaneLines(out, n, { areas: entries[n].areas, gates: entries[n].gates, parallelWith });
  }
  return out;
}

export function applyAnalysis(root, analysis, { phases, config = {}, now = new Date(), commit = (paths, message) => commitPaths(root, paths, message) } = {}) {
  const sc = scope(root, phases);
  if (!sc.targets.length) throw new AnnotateRefused(['nothing to mark up: every unfinished phase has fresh markup']);
  const problems = validateAnalysis(analysis, { targets: sc.targets, unfinished: sc.unfinished, gates: projectGates(config), deps: sc.deps });
  if (problems.length) throw new AnnotateRefused(problems);
  const given = new Map(Object.entries(analysis.phases).map(([n, e]) => [id(n), e]));
  const entries = {};
  for (const n of sc.unfinished) {
    const e = sc.targets.includes(n) ? given.get(n) : sc.raw?.phases[n];
    if (e) entries[n] = { areas: e.areas.map((m) => m.trim()), gates: [...new Set(e.gates)], confidence: e.confidence };
    else if (sc.targets.includes(n)) entries[n] = { areas: [EVERYTHING], gates: [], confidence: 'low' };
  }
  const low = sc.targets.filter((n) => !given.has(n));
  const pairs = mergePairs(sc.raw?.pairs ?? [], analysis.pairs ?? [], sc);
  const at = now.toISOString();
  const relax = (analysis.relax ?? []).map((r, i) => {
    const phase = id(r.phase);
    const drop = [...new Set(r.drop.map(id))];
    return { id: `r${i + 1}`, phase, drop, remaining: (sc.deps.get(phase) ?? []).filter((d) => !drop.includes(d)), kind: r.kind, reason: r.reason.trim(), from: sc.sections.get(phase)?.dependsOn ?? '' };
  });
  const max = config.autonomy === 'max';
  const deps = new Map(sc.deps);
  let text = sc.text;
  let proposal = sc.raw?.proposal ?? null;
  let asked = null;
  if (relax.length && max) {
    for (const r of relax) {
      text = setDependsOn(text, r.phase, r.remaining);
      deps.set(r.phase, r.remaining);
    }
    proposal = { id: null, state: 'applied', at, by: 'autonomy max', accepted: relax.map((r) => r.id), relax };
  } else if (relax.length) {
    asked = askCard(root, relax, config, now).question.id;
    // askOwner records needs-owner in pannotate.json, the annotate lane's record (L1): nothing waits for the card (D9)
    writeJobStatus(root, { kind: 'annotate', phase: null }, 'running', { reason: `dependency card ${asked} asked`, at });
    proposal = { id: asked, state: 'asked', at, by: null, accepted: [], relax };
  }
  text = markLines(text, entries, pairs, deps);
  fs.writeFileSync(roadmapFile(root), text);
  const adr = relax.length && max ? writeDecision(root, relax, { now, by: 'gsd-turbo (autonomy max)' }) : null;
  writeLanesFile(root, { phases: entries, pairs, proposal, annotatedAt: at });
  const c = commit([ROADMAP_REL, LANES_REL, ...(adr ? [adr] : [])], `docs(turbo): lane markup for phase ${sc.targets.join(', ')}`);
  return { annotated: sc.targets, low, pairs: pairs.length, relaxed: max ? relax.map((r) => r.id) : [], asked, adr, commit: c };
}

function closeQuestion(root, qid, now) {
  withPhaseLock(root, ANNOTATE_PHASE, () => {
    const list = readQuestions(root, ANNOTATE_PHASE);
    const q = list.find((x) => x.id === qid);
    if (!q || q.state !== 'answered') return;
    Object.assign(q, { state: 'delivered', delivery: { path: 'lane', at: now.toISOString() } });
    writeQuestions(root, ANNOTATE_PHASE, list);
  });
}

// D9: the owner's answer. A relaxation whose Depends on line changed since the card, or whose phase finished, is
// skipped, never overwritten. Section keys move only for phases that were fresh before, so staleness survives.
export function applyAnswer(root, choice, { phases, config = {}, now = new Date(), commit = (paths, message) => commitPaths(root, paths, message) } = {}) {
  const raw = readLanesRaw(root);
  const p = raw?.proposal;
  if (p?.state !== 'asked') throw new AnnotateRefused(['no dependency card waits for an answer']);
  if (!pendingAnswer(root, raw)) throw new AnnotateRefused([`the owner has not answered ${p.id} yet`]);
  const ids = choice === 'all' ? p.relax.map((r) => r.id) : choice === 'none' ? [] : choice;
  const unknown = ids.filter((x) => !p.relax.some((r) => r.id === x));
  if (unknown.length) throw new AnnotateRefused([`no proposal ${unknown.join(', ')}: the card holds ${p.relax.map((r) => r.id).join(', ')}`]);
  const sc = scope(root, phases);
  const deps = new Map(sc.deps);
  let text = sc.text;
  const applied = [];
  const skipped = [];
  for (const r of p.relax.filter((x) => ids.includes(x.id))) {
    const s = sc.sections.get(r.phase);
    if (!s || !sc.unfinished.includes(r.phase) || (s.dependsOn ?? '') !== r.from) {
      skipped.push(r.id);
      continue;
    }
    const remaining = (deps.get(r.phase) ?? []).filter((d) => !r.drop.includes(d));
    text = setDependsOn(text, r.phase, remaining);
    deps.set(r.phase, remaining);
    applied.push({ ...r, remaining });
  }
  const entries = {};

}
```

````markdown
name: turbo-lane-analyst
description: Marks up a GSD milestone's unfinished phases so gsd-turbo can run independent phases at the same time. Reads the brief of turbo-run annotate brief (the ROADMAP, each phase's CONTEXT, RESEARCH and plans, the code) and writes one analysis JSON - per phase the path masks it may touch (Areas), the shared resources it holds (Gates) and a confidence; the pairs of phases that may or may not run together; and dependency relaxations, only for Depends on lines that are prose or imaginary. tools: Read, Grep, Glob, Bash, Write

You are turbo-lane-analyst, a gsd-turbo agent. The prompt gives you the path of a brief (JSON) and the path to write your analysis to. Nobody watches you in real time, and you cannot ask questions.

## Never

- Never write any file but the analysis file. Never edit the ROADMAP, a plan, code or a config: turbo-run annotate apply writes the markup from your analysis.
- Never commit, push or run a command that changes the repository or GSD's state. Bash is for reading only: `git log`, `git show`, `git grep`, `git ls-files`.
- Never spawn other agents.
- Never add a dependency, and never drop one through which the phases share files, data or behaviour.

## The brief

- `targets`: the phases to mark up, each with `phase`, `name`, `goal`, `deps` (the dependencies GSD reads), `dependsOn` (the Depends on line as written), and the paths of its `context`, `research` and `plans` (each plan with its `filesModified`).
- `others`: the other unfinished phases, with the markup they already have (`areas`, `gates`, `confidence`).
- `gates`: the gate names you may use. `lang`: the owner's language. `roadmap`: the ROADMAP path. `analysisFile`: where you write.

## Procedure

1. Read the brief, the ROADMAP section of every target, and each target's CONTEXT, RESEARCH and plans when they exist.
2. **Areas.** For each target, the files it may create, change or delete: source, tests, fixtures, configs, lockfiles, docs. Start from the plans' `filesModified`, then the CONTEXT and RESEARCH, then the code (`git ls-files`, `git grep` for the modules the phase names). Write path masks from the repository root: `*` matches within one path segment, `**` any number of segments (`src/auth/**`, `test/auth-*.test.mjs`, `package.json`). Forward slashes; no `{a,b}` braces (write each alternative as its own mask); no commas; no absolute paths; no `..`; never `.planning/**` (each phase has its own directory there). Err broad: a mask that misses a file lets two phases change it at the same time, while a broad one only costs parallelism. A phase that may change a shared file (`package.json`, a lockfile, a central config, a schema) lists it.
3. **Confidence.** `high` when the plans, or the CONTEXT and the code, name what the phase changes. `low` when you cannot tell (no CONTEXT, no plans and a vague goal): turbo then counts the phase as everything (`**`), whatever its areas.
4. **Gates.** The shared resources the phase holds for its whole run, from `gates` only: `prod-deploy` (deploys to a server or production), `owner-live` (needs the owner's live session, accounts or device), `owner-signature` (the owner signs or decides), `packaged-build` (builds or packages the installable app), and any project gate the brief lists. None: `[]`.
5. **Pairs.** For every target and every other unfinished phase (targets and `others`): `conflict` when their areas may share a file, when they share a gate, or when one changes data or behaviour the other relies on; else `ok`. One short reason each. Judge files, gates, data and behaviour only: declared dependencies are checked by the scheduler itself.
6. **Relaxations** (most milestones have none). A target's dependency may be dropped only when it is `prose` (the Depends on line names the phase only in wording that is no precondition, for example "Must land before Phase 28", which GSD reads as a dependency on phase 28) or `imaginary` (the two phases share no files, no data and no behaviour, so their order does not matter). Name the phases to drop in `drop`, and give the reason in one or two sentences in the brief's `lang`.
7. Write the analysis to `analysisFile`, then reply in at most 5 lines: the phases, how many pairs are `ok`, any `low` confidence, any relaxation.

## The analysis

```json
{
  "phases": {
    "2": { "areas": ["src/api/**", "test/api/**", "package.json"], "gates": [], "confidence": "high" },
    "3": { "areas": ["web/**", "test/web/**"], "gates": ["prod-deploy"], "confidence": "high" }
  },
  "pairs": [["2", "3", "conflict", "both change package.json"]],
  "relax": [{ "phase": "3", "drop": ["2"], "kind": "prose", "reason": "the Depends on line only says the web client comes later in the story" }]
}
```

`phases` holds every target and nothing else. Each `pairs` entry is `[phase, phase, "conflict" or "ok", reason]` and names at least one target. `relax` may be `[]`. turbo-run annotate apply checks all of it and refuses with a list of problems; when you are dispatched again with that list, fix exactly those.
````

```markdown
## The step loop

Repeat:

1. `turbo-run phase-step N --prep` prints `phase N: prep next <step>`. On `next none`: **Finish**.
2. Context: `turbo-run context N`. 3. Run the step's section of `/turbo-phase` with the changes above.
4. `turbo-run phase-step N --prep --done <step> --note "<one line: what happened>"`.

## Finish

1. `git status --porcelain`. A change inside the phase directory → commit it: `gsd-tools commit "docs(phase-N): prep" --files <those paths>`. 2. `turbo-run lane-status N prepped --job prep`.
   - Exit 0: end your turn.
   - Exit 1 with `refused: outside the phase directory of phase N: <paths> (base <sha>); …`: for each path run `git cat-file -e <sha>:<path>`; exit 0 → `git checkout <sha> -- <path>`; otherwise → `git rm -q -- <path>`.    - Exit 1 with any other `refused: …` (for example `phase N has no CONTEXT.md: prep is not done`) → **Stop** with that line.

Wait until every subagent you started has finished and its result has arrived. - The records are L1's (L1 plan D3): `readLaneStatus(root, recordKey(lane))`, i.e. `p<N>.json` for a prep job and `pannotate.json` for the annotate job, `LANE_STATUSES` holding `prepped` and `annotated`. `JOB_STATUSES = { prep: ['running', 'paused-context', 'failed', 'prepped'], annotate: ['running', 'paused-context', 'failed', 'annotated'] }`; `jobKey(job)` = `prep-<N>` | `annotate` (= L0's lanes key); `jobRecordKey(job)` = `<N>` | `annotate` (= L1's `recordKey`); `writeJobStatus(root, job, status, { reason = '', at }) → record` (`writeLaneStatus`'s shape, after checking the status against the kind); `readJobStatus(root, job) → record | null`; `writeAnnotateAttempt(root, { status, roadmapSha256, at })` / `readAnnotateAttempt(root)` (`run/annotate-attempt.json`, written with every annotate status).
```
- The job sessions write them with `turbo-run lane-status <N> <status> --job prep` and `turbo-run lane-status annotate <status>` (`jobLaneStatus`, `lib/cli-jobs.mjs`); `prepped`/`annotated` are refused (exit 1) unless the slot is clean, the branch's net diff from the slot record's `job.base.sha` passes `allowedFor(job)`, a prep has CONTEXT.md and an annotate's `lanes.json` is fresh.
- L1: `prepped`/`annotated` (fresh, turn ended) → `finishJob`; `paused-context`, or a session gone without a final status → relaunch through `jobPrompts({ …, resume: true })` within `max_restarts_without_progress`; `failed`, or restarts used up → L1 writes `writeJobStatus(root, job, 'failed', { reason })` (when the record does not say it yet), drops the lanes entry and calls `releaseSlot(root, slot, { outcome: 'failed' })`. A failed prep notifies nobody (it only costs time); a failed annotate notifies the owner once. A job never records `needs-owner` (the markup's card does not stop it: below). `pendingJobs` never queues a prep whose record reads `failed` or `prepped` again, nor an annotate that failed on the same ROADMAP digest.

**Planning-only merges — L2's `mergePlanningOnly(ctx, lane, { allowed })` (`lib/merge.mjs`)**
- `allowedFor(job) → (relPath: string) => boolean` (`lib/prep.mjs`); `prepAllowed(phase)` — true only for a file below `.planning/phases/<a directory whose GSD token is N>/`; `annotateAllowed()` — true only for `.planning/ROADMAP.md`, `.planning/turbo/lanes.json`, `.planning/turbo/DECISIONS.md` and files below `.planning/decisions/`. Both refuse empty, `.` and `..` segments and read backslashes as `/`. `allowedFor` throws for any other kind.
- `finishJob` (above) calls it as `mergePlanningOnly(ctx, lane, { allowed: allowedFor({ kind: lane.job, phase: lane.phase }) })`. L2 applies `allowed` to every path the branch changed since its merge base (`git diff --name-only --no-renames`, the net change: a commit that changed STATE.md and a later one that put it back pass) and refuses the whole merge when any path fails; no tests, no `phase.complete`, no push (spec §7.2 last line); it does not release the slot (`finishJob` does).
- `outOfScope(root, job, { base, git }) → { dirty: string[], outside: string[] }` and `finishBlocker(root, job, deps) → string | null` (`lib/cli-jobs.mjs`) are the job-side check, for L2 to reuse in tests if useful.

**Plan-only start — L1**
- `/turbo-plan-milestone` runs `turbo-run start --plan-only [--from <N>] [--to <N>] | --only <N> | --all`. L1 implements the flag: kept in `supervisor.json` as `planOnly: true` (kept by a daemon restart, cleared by a later `start` without it); while it is set the scheduler launches no phase lane, only `pendingJobs`; the daemon ends, as at milestone end, once `pendingJobs` returns `[]` and no prep or annotate lane runs, with one owner notification (en «Milestone prepared», ru «Майлстоун подготовлен»). Outside the flag, `start` and its output are unchanged.

**The dependency card — L1, L4**
- With `autonomy: standard` the markup asks one card under the pseudo-phase `ANNOTATE_PHASE` (`annotate`): `run/pannotate-questions.json`, answers in `.planning/turbo/answers/pannotate.json`, answered like any question (`turbo-run answer annotate ask-<k> …`, the pane, Telegram). Nothing waits for it: the markup job ends, and `pendingJobs` queues a new annotate job once the answer is in; that job applies it and closes the question (`delivered`, path `lane`). L1 wakes no session for it and never treats `annotate` as a phase (no phase lane, no `attend`). `askOwner` writes `needs-owner` into `run/pannotate.json` (the annotate lane's record); `applyAnalysis` puts `running` back at once, and the session ends with `annotated`. L4 shows the card like any question, titled as the lane markup (en «Lane markup», ru «Разметка полос») instead of «Phase annotate».
